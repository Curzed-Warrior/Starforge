How to watch

A spacefaring civilization has arrived in a star system it has never seen. It makes its own decisions: what to research, which worlds to mine or settle, what to do about anyone already living here, and whether to answer when something calls.

Its personality shapes everything. Its drive (Expansionist, Contemplative, Industrious) belongs to whichever faction holds the council, and changes when elections or schisms do. Its ethics (Reverent, Pragmatic, Ruthless) drift with what it does.

Watching

  • Autoplay keeps the story going on its own: milestone banners close themselves, and once a seed ship has left and the work here is done, the chronicle follows it to the next star.
  • Auto camera cuts to wherever something is happening. Drag or pinch to take over; it resumes after a while.
  • Chronicle holds the whole history, with charts. Development shows what it is researching and what it cares about.
  • Tap a planet, or the flotilla's label, to see what the council thinks of it.

Interfering

  • Inspire a direction in Development, send a comet of raw material, or trigger a flare. Each has a cooldown, and the civilization reacts to it.
  • On a planet card you can order a world mined, or protect a native species.
  • During a crisis, Intervene ends it.

The goal, if it has one, is Kardashev Type II: capturing almost all of a star's output. After that, each drive has its own great project, and seed ships can carry the civilization to a new star.
